⚠️ Common Cyber Threats

🎣 Phishing

Fake emails or messages designed to steal passwords and personal information.

🦠 Malware

Malicious software that can damage systems or steal sensitive information.

🔒 Ransomware

Malware that locks or encrypts files and demands payment from the victim.

🐴 Trojan

A malicious program that appears to be legitimate software.

🛡️ Cyber Security Tips

  • Use strong and unique passwords.
  • Enable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA).
  • Do not click suspicious links.
  • Keep your software updated.
  • Do not share OTP or passwords.
  • Use trusted websites and applications.
